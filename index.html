[~45s] Not everything is a list. When a slide needs a diagram that animates, write it as an HTML section next to the Markdown. Register its walkthrough with Deck.steps, and it takes part in the same navigation: down arrow, the step counter, the URL. In read view, this slide appears as a figure you can tap to present.
